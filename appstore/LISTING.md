## Description

Head to Head lays out sprinkler systems for landscapers and homeowners. Walk the edge of the lawn
with your iPhone's camera and the app traces it as you go. No measuring wheel, no tape, no drawing
it up later. You can also trace the yard on a satellite photo or on grid paper.

Then tap Generate. Head to Head places every head the way an installer would: rotors in the open,
pop-ups along curves and narrow spots, strip nozzles on park strips, heads set head to head for
double coverage, and no spray over straight edges like sidewalks and fences. It splits the heads
into zones that fit your water pressure and supply line, and keeps each zone in one part of the
yard so the trenching stays simple.

You get:

- A to-scale plan for the customer and one for the crew, ready to print or save as a PDF
- A parts list for the counter: spray bodies, nozzles, rotors, pipe, fittings, valves and boxes
- Trench and pipe lengths, drip zones for the beds, and sod pallets for the lawn
- Trees, fences, beds, the meter and spigots taken into account

For contractors: jobs save online so your whole crew can open them. Your plan covers every yard.

For homeowners: map your own yard for free and see how many heads and zones it needs. Unlock the
full layout, plan and parts list one yard at a time.

Built by a landscaper who ran a sprinkler supply counter.

Head to Head gives an estimate from the map you draw. Check measurements, water pressure and local
codes, and call 811 before you dig.
