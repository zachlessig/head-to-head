Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Head to Head App LLC ("Head to Head", "we", "us") collects when you use the Head to Head website or iPhone app, why, and who else handles it. The short version: we collect what it takes to map a yard, save it, and take a payment. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

1. What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign in. We send a 6-digit code to it so you can sign in without a password.
  • Your company: its name, the people on it and their email addresses, and invites you send.
  • Your yards and jobs: the shapes you draw or walk, markers like trees and the water meter, the address if you add one, pressure and supply details, and the layouts, plans and parts lists made from them. For contractors these are saved online so your crew can open them. Homeowners who don't sign in keep their yards on their own device.
  • Your location, only when you tap "Use where I am now" and allow it. It's used to open the map on the yard you're standing in and look up the street address. We don't track your location in the background.
  • The camera, only during a camera walk in the iPhone app. The video is processed on your phone to trace the edge of the lawn. We don't record, upload or keep any video or photos.
  • Payment records: which plan or yard you paid for and when. Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

2. How we use it

To run the Service: sign you in, save and sync your jobs, lay out sprinklers, show your plan and billing, and answer you when you write to us. We may use anonymous, combined information, with nothing that identifies you or an address, to improve how the Service lays out yards.

3. Who else handles it

These companies process data for us, only to run the Service:

  • Supabase stores accounts, companies and jobs (servers in the United States).
  • Stripe takes payments and keeps billing records.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Mapbox and Esri provide the satellite map, address search and address lookup. Searches and map views send them the area or address being looked up.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare host the website and its domain.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising. We'll share it if the law requires us to.

4. How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. When you use Delete my account (on the company screen, in the app or on the website), your sign-in is deleted, and if you own a company, the company and all of its jobs are deleted with it. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

5. Your choices

  • Location and camera are off unless you allow them, and you can turn them off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

6. Kids

Head to Head is for contractors and homeowners. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each company can only see its own jobs. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us right away if you think your account has been misused.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced in the app or by email.
